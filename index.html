Features

Everything you need to stay on track

Seven powerful features that turn a simple list into your personal productivity system.

Sign up & Log in

Create an optional account so your tasks are saved across sessions and devices.

Task Management

Create, update and delete tasks, each with a title, description and deadline.

Prioritization

Mark tasks as low, medium or high priority so you always know what comes first.

Deadlines & Reminders

Set deadlines and get reminders before your upcoming tasks are due.

Categories

Group your tasks into Work, Personal and School, or create your own.

Filter & Sort

Sort tasks by deadline, priority or category and find anything in seconds.

Admin Dashboard

Admins monitor active users and generate reports on task completion rates and app usage.
